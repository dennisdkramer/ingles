Numbers, dates, money and data

Numbers are read in groups of three

Os números são lidos em grupos de três: 1,500 = one thousand five hundred; 2.4 million = two point four million. Hundred, thousand e million são as únicas palavras de grupo que você precisa.

Hundred, thousand and million are the only group words you need

English groups a long number in threes from the right, and each group carries one word: hundred, thousand, million. 1,200,000 is one million two hundred thousand.

After a number the group word stays singular: two hundred, three thousand, five million — never two hundreds. The plural belongs to an uncounted many: hundreds of people.

A decimal is said with point, not comma: 2.4 is two point four. 1,200 is one thousand two hundred — American English usually leaves out the and.

Dates: the fifth, and the year

A data é dita com um ordinal e leva on: on May fifth, on the fifth of May. O dia do mês não é o número do cardinal, e sim o ordinal — o "quinto", não o "cinco".

A date is said with an ordinal, and it takes on

A date uses the ordinal: first, second, third, fourth, fifth … The written form May 5 is said May fifth. When the month is already known, the day alone is enough: on the fifth.

The day keeps on from lesson 6: The invoice is due on the fifth. Due means the payment has to arrive by that date. An invoice is the document that asks a client to pay.

The year has two normal readings: 2026 is twenty twenty-six (or two thousand twenty-six); 2019 is twenty nineteen; 1900 is nineteen hundred.

The months are the same words you already know with a capital letter: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

Times: half past two

A hora leva at: at nine, at noon. E half past two é metade depois das duas — 2:30, o mesmo que two thirty, que é mais curto e muito comum na fala.

At takes the clock, and half past is thirty minutes after the hour

A clock time takes at, the point from lesson 6: The call is at half past two. Half past two is 2:30 — thirty minutes after two, not half of two.

2:15 is a quarter past two; 2:45 is a quarter to three. A quarter is fifteen minutes, and a minute is the smallest unit the clock shows.

An hour is sixty minutes and a day is twenty-four hours; a week is seven days and a month is the page of the calendar — the four weeks are not exact, so English counts months, not weeks, in a year.

The meeting is — not half of two.

half past twoduas e meia — thirty minutes after two, so 2:30. A quarter to three is 2:45; a quarter past two is 2:15.

The same clock, written and said:

  • 2:00 — at two
  • 2:15 — at a quarter past two
  • 2:30 — at half past two
  • 2:45 — at a quarter to three

Money and parts: half, a quarter, the price

A parte de um todo e o dinheiro que a paga: half = metade, a quarter = um quarto, e o preço se diz com it costs…. Estas palavras também aparecem em números de reunião.

A part of a whole, and the money that pays for it

half is fifty percent and a quarter is twenty-five: It's about a quarter of the budget. Half the report is ready. The words work in front of the with no preposition: half the report, not the half of the report.

price is what something costs: the price is a dollar fifty for $1.50. A price is a point on a scale, so it takes at from lesson 6: at that price.

Money also takes for to name what it is for: an invoice for $1,500, a cheque for $200. That is the same contrast as lesson 6: for is the purpose; to is the destination.

Three amounts the same way:

  • half — 50%: half the budget
  • a quarter — 25%: a quarter of the budget
  • a dollar fifty — $1.50, the price

A quarter of needs of; half does not: half the report, a quarter of the report.

Figures that move: from 1.2 to 1.5 million

Números que se movem: nomeia os dois pontos e depois a direção — from 1.2 to 1.5 million, up or down. O from … to … é o mesmo do português "de … para …".

Name both ends, then the direction

To describe a change, name the two figures with from and to: Sales are up from 1.2 to 1.5 million. Costs go down in Q3.

up and down give the direction; from … to give the two ends; by gives the size of the move: up by 300,000.

Q3 is the third quarter: the year in four parts. It takes in, a period of time, the same way July did in lesson 6: in Q3.

Say the unit once, after the last figure: from 1.2 to 1.5 million, not from 1.2 million to 1.5 million.

The same move, said three ways:

  • Sales are up from 1.2 to 1.5 million.
  • Sales are up by 300,000.
  • Sales go up in Q3.

The first names both ends; the second names the size; the third names the direction and the period.
