The Sounds — Vowels, Consonants & the Key

Today you get the two halves of one skill: the sounds (what your mouth does) and the key (how to read a word you have never seen and commit to a pronunciation without asking anyone). This is a double lesson — ears first, then the mouth, then the key. Esta é a aula mais longa do bloco: ouça tudo antes de ler, e não tente decorar o mapa de uma vez.

3. The vowel map — name, big, small

Every vowel has a name (what you say when you spell it), a big sound (the name, said clearly) and a small sound (short, relaxed — sometimes it disappears into ə). The letter does not decide; the word does.

VowelNameBig soundSmall sound
Aeimake · nameman · cat
Eime · seemen · bed
Iaimine · timesit · ship
Oougo · nohot · stop
Uiucute · musiccup · mum

O som pequeno de I não existe em português: é um "i" curto e relaxado, entre "e" e "i" (sit, ship, minute). É o som que mais entrega o sotaque brasileiro. The small U is already the schwa.

4. The key — how a word really sounds

The key is an X-ray of the spoken word. Learn to read it once and you can decode any word in the dictionary.
