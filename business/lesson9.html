BUSINESS LESSON 9

There is / There are & Place

Abertura: Hoje você diz o que existe no escritório com there is / there are, localiza as coisas com in / on / at (lugar), e conecta as ideias com and / but / because / so. Três ferramentas que deixam a sua fala mais completa.

1. There is / There are — há, existe

There is + singular · There are + plural. Pergunta: inverte → Is there…? / Are there…? Negativa: There isn't / There aren't.

UsoExemplo
singularThere's a meeting room.
pluralThere are three meeting rooms.
perguntaIs there a printer?
negativaThere isn't a printer.
⚠️ O verbo concorda com o que vem depois: there is + singular, there are + plural.

2. in / on / at — lugar

in = dentro de um lugar maior; on = em cima / superfície; at = um ponto / alvo.

PreposiçãoExemplo
in (dentro)in the office · in Recife · in the box
on (superfície)on the desk · on the wall · on the table
at (ponto)at the door · at reception · at home
⚠️ at the office = no local de trabalho; in the office = dentro do prédio.

3. and / but / because / so — conectores

and = e (soma); but = mas (contraste); because = porque (razão); so = então (resultado).

ConectorExemplo
and (e)It's small and practical.
but (mas)It's small but practical.
because (porque)I'm late because of traffic.
so (então)It's raining, so I'll stay.
