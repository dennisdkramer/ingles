I Understand Your Concern

What this lesson installs: the service script and de-escalation. The first law is a sequence: greet → identify the problem → own it → fix it → close, always in that order. And the first sentence never carries information: it carries empathy — I understand your concern. Let me see what I can do. The second law: never a "no" on its own — always the partial yes: what I can do today is… The third: escalating is promising with a date — I'll escalate this to senior management and come back to you by Friday, with a name, a path (escalation path) and a deadline; a promise without a date becomes the second complaint. At the end, the follow-up that turns a complaint into retention.

1. The service script

Five stages, each with one job. Skipping a stage is audible even when the words are right — and the one that gets skipped is almost always the first. Listen ▶ in order.

StageFormulaIn Portuguese
1. greetGood morning, you're speaking to Ana.bom dia, é a Ana quem fala
2. identifyCan you tell me what happened?pode me contar o que aconteceu?
3. own itI'm sorry this happened — I'll take care of it.sinto muito; vou cuidar disso
4. fix itWhat I can do today is send a replacement.o que posso fazer hoje é enviar outro
5. closeI'll follow up by e-mail today.vou dar retorno por e-mail hoje
own it is not confessing fault: I'm sorry this happened takes responsibility for the experience, not necessarily for the failure. That is what satisfies the customer without handing over the company — and the difference is a choice of words, not of feeling.

A. Which stage is it?

1."You're speaking to Ana."
2."Can you tell me what happened?"
3."I'll take care of it."
4."I'll follow up by e-mail today."

2. De-escalation

The sentence that lowers the temperature is not the one that explains: it is the one that names what the other person is feeling. It comes before any information. Listen ▶.

FormulaWhat it doesIn Portuguese
I understand your concern.names the feelingentendo a sua preocupação
I can see why that's frustrating.acknowledges the effectvejo por que isso irrita
Let me see what I can do.buys time without promisingdeixe-me ver o que posso fazer
Thank you for your patience.acknowledges the other person's waitobrigado pela paciência
Never open with actually, unfortunately or a policy. The order is person → problem → policy. Whoever opens with the policy loses the customer before finding out what they wanted.

A. What is its job?

1.I understand your concern.
2.Let me see what I can do.
3.Thank you for your patience.
4.I can see why that's frustrating.

3. The "no" that keeps the customer

Never a no on its own. The partial yes has three parts: what is not possible, what is possible today, and what happens next. Listen ▶.

SituationThe partial yesWhy
a refund request outside policyI can't refund it, but what I can do today is send a replacement.it refuses the impossible and offers the possible
a delivery that will not arrive tomorrowTomorrow isn't possible. I can get it to you by Friday.it swaps an impossible date for a real one
a request to speak to the directorI'll escalate it and come back to you by Friday.it does not refuse access; it gives a route
a customer who wants compensationI can't promise compensation, but I can offer free delivery next time.it separates what cannot be done from what can
The structure is always can't… but what I can do is… The but is not decoration: it is what signals that the conversation continues. Without the second half the customer hears only "no" — and their next step is your competitor.

4. Escalating is promising with a date

Escalating is not passing the problem on: it is opening a route and guaranteeing the return. Four pieces — what, to whom, when, and how the customer finds out. Listen ▶.

PieceFormulaIn Portuguese
whatI'll raise a ticket for the missing delivery.vou abrir um chamado
to whomIt goes to our logistics team first.vai primeiro para a logística
whenYou'll hear from us by Friday at the latest.você terá notícia até sexta
howI'll send you the reference number today.mando o número do protocolo hoje
The vocabulary of the field: complaint (reclamação), escalation path (o caminho de quem trata o quê), service recovery (o conserto que recupera o cliente), goodwill (a compensação de boa vontade), retention (o cliente que fica).

A. Which piece is it?

1.I'll raise a ticket for it.
2.You'll hear from us by Friday.
3.It goes to logistics first.
4.I'll send the reference number.

5. The call

A delivery that never arrived. Read it, listen ▶, and label the stage of each line the agent says.

Ana: Good morning, you're speaking to Ana. How can I help?

Client: My order was due on Monday and nothing arrived.

Ana: I'm sorry this happened. Can you tell me the order number?

Ana: Thank you. I can see why that's frustrating. What I can do today is send a replacement.

Client: I want a refund, not a replacement.

Ana: I can't refund it, but I can escalate it to my manager and you'll hear from us by Friday.

Ana: I'll send the reference number today and follow up on Friday.

A. Which stage is it?

1."Good morning, you're speaking to Ana."
2."I'm sorry this happened."
3."What I can do today is send a replacement."
4."You'll hear from us by Friday."
