## Decisions (ambiguities hit while generating - resolved here, not silently)

   - **Block names are not given anywhere.** Decided: 1-24 *Working foundations* (the student can run a simple workday alone); 26-44 *Professional depth* (time depth, hypotheticals, multi-clause output, workplace functions); 46-50 *Natural and informal English* (polish only, nothing after depends on it).
   - **Number of families.** PEDAGOGY.md names mechanisms but never counts families. Decided: a family is one mechanism, contrast or rule that a lesson can finish - so vocabulary themes (meetings, email, data) are NOT families, they are carrier sets inside the lessons that teach a function. Hence 36 spine lines and labels spread across 42 areas.
   - **Labels per teaching lesson.** The generator allows 3-6. Fixed at 3 per teaching lesson: 3 labels plus 10-12 items is a lesson; pushing to 5-6 forced padding with a title or an activity, which the generator forbids as a label.
   - **Vocabulary size.** Floor is 10. Fixed at 10-12 items per teaching lesson so no lesson is only vocabulary.
   - **Formal twins of phrasal verbs** (`arrange`, `postpone`, `cancel`, `review`) are never labels, never the default answer, never vocabulary items: PEDAGOGY.md makes the phrasal verb the taught form and the Latin word a gloss. Where the twin itself is the rule it is taught as the formal/casual axis (`axis/written-vs-spoken`), not as a verb list.
   - **Transparent + marginal, with a recorded reason (PEDAGOGY.md requires one).** Numbers, dates, money and data go at lesson 10, not late: the persona analyses figures daily, and the cognate density makes this the cheapest progress in block 1.
   - **The informal material.** `BUSINESS-REGISTER.md` c) is never a lesson's default. It enters as the pairing mechanism at lesson 39 (`informal/sidenote-pairing`) and as its own tail lesson 50, because an informal twin is only teachable once its business-casual twin exists (the same logic PEDAGOGY.md uses for the formal gloss).
   - **No lesson is assigned a register anywhere in this file**, and the word does not appear as an in-content label: the formal/casual axis carries that work.
   - **`get` is two labels, not one.** `get/wildcard` (lesson 7: receive, obtain, become, arrive, understand) and `phrase-verb/root-family-get` (lesson 18: get up / get back / get on / get over), because a student can fail the general senses while passing "get back to".
   - **Exams.** 25 and 45 own no `teaches:` and no `vocabulary:`; they carry `Focus:` (how to test) and `uses:` (which earlier labels are drawn on).
   - **Split topics are two labels** (`question/embedded-order` + `question/embedded-wh`), never one label spread over two lessons.
