Games — preview

Not a lesson. Every game engine4.js can present, one per heading, with content shaped for that game's own mechanism instead of one item list copied into twelve blocks. The authored .game-list is what a student sees before Start, what prints, and what is saved; everything around it is presentation.

Every block is <div class="game" data-game="…"> over plain .qitems: a .qstem prompt, optional .listen chip, radios. data-a on the item is the right option's value; data-answer carries the word itself only where a board needs it (wordleBoard, crosswordBoard, the bingo caller).
