A Four-Minute Mistake

One page, every mechanism. Structure decides the arrangement, the control decides what the student does, the look decides the paint. Any combination is valid, and anything unset falls back to the plain version of itself.

1 · Structures that arrange

The shift

Marcos clocked in at 6:40 and found the loading bay already full. Nobody had told him about the delivery, so he stacked the pallets where the forklift turns.

By 6:44 one of them was on the floor of the bay, and the bolt that holds the rack was somewhere under a hundred boxes of paper towels.

Words from the shift

  • clock inregister your arrival at work
  • baythe open area where trucks unload
  • palleta stack of goods on a wooden base
  • forkliftthe machine that lifts pallets
  • manifestthe list of what a truck carries
  • tip overfall on its side

Why the order matters

The accident is not the interesting part. What matters is that the same four minutes can be told as a sequence of events, as a list of words, or as one picture with a caption — and the student needs all three, in that order, before the past continuous makes any sense.

Structure is chosen by what the reader must do with the information, not by what it looks like.

7 · Reading support

Meaning behind a click, key above the word

He clocked in at 6:40 —

clock inBater o ponto. Register your arrival. Entrar no relógio is not what the phrase says: the clock is the record, not the door. Opposite: clock out.

They record it, then write it in the record.

record(ri-córd) the verb, record(ré-cord) the noun — the key sits over the word, the dot marks the loud syllable, and neither replaces the other.

Reading mode

The switch in the bar is a plain checkbox. When it is checked, the page narrows to 820px and every block reflows to the new width.

No class on the body, no script, no second stylesheet — one rule reads the checkbox with :has().

This block carries data-motion="rise". Its arrival is tied to where it sits in the viewport, not to a timer.

What is deliberately absent

No parallax, no infinite animation, no cursor effect, no script. Every motion on this page is CSS, settles in under half a second, and stops entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.
