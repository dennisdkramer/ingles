# Lesson 1 — design, second independent pass (Pass A, stability check)

## Core
1. Say who they are, where they are from and what they do, with the subject always spoken.
2. Ask the same three things back, and understand the answer at speed.
3. Put *a/an* in front of a noun by sound.
4. Say the time and the weather with *it*.
5. Hold the final consonant and pitch the first puff of *p/t/k*.

## Situation
A video call with four new colleagues. Everyone introduces themselves in one line; then someone asks the time and someone asks where the student is from.

## Candidates
- **Heard — the roll call.** Five one-line introductions; the student matches each voice to a name card.
- **Said — my three lines.** The student records the same three sentences three ways: to a peer, to a manager, to a client (only *Hi* vs *Good morning* changes).
- **Done — the sound check.** Two-word pairs (*sit/sat*, *ship/sheep*) as a physical warm-up before the sentences.

## Chosen
**Roll call → my three lines → sound check.** The repeat-recording is the spine because it makes the student say the target six times with a real audience in mind, and it exposes the final consonant (the word *manager* is where they will hear themselves fail).

**First 30 seconds:** five voices introduce themselves; the student drags nothing, just ticks names — they are listening to English before being asked to speak a word.

## Evidence
Name matches, the three recordings, *a/an* choices, one listening check on the sound pairs, and a quiz.

## Conflicts
- Recording three times is heavy for a first lesson; resolution: one recording, two takes allowed.
- This pass drops the *it* + weather half of the entry to a single line of practice — flagged, because it is the weakest-motivated part at A0.

## Notes for the build pass
PT for everything except the targets. Record early, table late.
