# PHONEMES — multi-consonant units (reconstructed 2026-10-27)

## Purpose
Teach RHYTHM to beginner Brazilian students. The visual key groups consonant clusters as ONSETS so
students learn to "mash" them together (natural English rhythm) instead of saying each letter.

## Parsing principle
1. Keep a consonant cluster whole as an onset when it is a genuine onset.
2. Split it at a true word/compound boundary (rat-race, mis-treat, his-tri-o-nic).
3. "Both ways" (ret-ro-fit vs re-tro-fit) → choose the onset grouping that teaches rhythm (re-tro-fit).
   Rhythm WINS over etymology in gray cases (mi-sfit, not mis-fit).

## Split / no-split / both — worked examples
| unit | must split | must not split | both ways (choose rhythm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| tr | rat-race | re-try | re-tro-fit (over ret-ro-fit) |
| st | mis-treat | a-stray | — |
| sf | — | mi-sfit (mash s+f) | — |
| nd | in-deed | — | — |
| cl | — | a-clue, un-cle | — |

## Notes
- Vowel digraphs/trigraphs (ea ee oa ai oo igh eigh ough augh) are vowel NUCLEI — never split, and NOT in these lists.
- Irregular vowel spellings (augh/ough/eigh) are a VOWEL problem, taught by lesson prose + ▶ audio, not by the break key. They may need manual core/hanging treatment in the columns 4–6 task.
