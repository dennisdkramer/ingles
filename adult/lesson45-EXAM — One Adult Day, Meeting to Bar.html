EXAM — One Adult Day, Meeting to Bar

No labels today. One day, five scenes, and every scene crosses three or four of the families.

09:00 — the meeting

Report what the boss said

Shift the tense, keep the meaning

Marina is telling you what the director said twenty minutes ago. Fill each gap with the shifted form — and notice that the obligations come with the same force they had in his mouth.

  1. He said the deadline to Friday.
  2. He told us we cut two features — no choice, he said.
  3. He asked we were still on the call.
  4. He mentioned that the client already approved the budget.
  5. And he said we have the numbers before he lands.

Gap 2 is the obligation family in reported form, gap 3 is the yes-or-no bridge, and gap 5 is the warning — the same line lesson 31 taught, one tense back.

A perfect tense for what is done, a relative clause for who is who

Gap 4 is the duration perfect: since plus a point in time. Gap 5 is the softener plus the reason, which is the whole work register in one line.
